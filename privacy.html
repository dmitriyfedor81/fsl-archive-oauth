Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

1. Application Purpose

FSL rclone archive is a private, single-user application used to back up, verify, and restore Futures Signal Lab research data through Google Drive using rclone.

2. Google Drive Access

The application accesses Google Drive only with authorization from its owner. Access is used for managing backup files, checking their integrity, and restoring previously stored archives.

3. Data Storage

Backup files are stored in the owner's Google Drive account and on systems controlled by the owner.

4. Data Sharing

The application is not offered to external users. It does not sell personal data or intentionally share Google user data with third parties.

5. Security

OAuth credentials and access tokens are not published on this website. Authorization is managed through Google's OAuth system and rclone.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

The owner controls backup retention and may delete stored archives. Google Drive access can be revoked through Google Account settings.

7. Contact

For privacy inquiries, contact the application owner using the support email listed on the application's Google OAuth consent screen.
